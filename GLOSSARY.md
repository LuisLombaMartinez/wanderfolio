# Viajecito

A private photo album of the trips two partners have made, or are planning, together. One partner can invite the other to a new trip with a playful invitation.

## People

**Member**:
One of the exactly two people who own the album. Both Members have equal rights over everything in it.
_Avoid_: User, account, admin, owner

## Album

**Album**:
The collection of all Trips a Member can see, newest first.
_Avoid_: Gallery, feed, timeline

**Trip**:
A journey the Members make together, with a title, a destination, start and end dates, an optional description and its Photos. It is **upcoming** until its start date and **past** after that.
_Avoid_: Travel, journey, holiday, memory, place

**Photo**:
An image of a Trip uploaded by a Member, with an optional caption and the date it was taken.
_Avoid_: Picture, image, media, asset

**Cover photo**:
The one Photo of a Trip chosen to represent it in the Album.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, hero image

## Invitations

**Invitation**:
A playful yes/no question one Member sends the other to invite them on a Trip. A Trip has at most one. Its "No" can never be chosen.
_Avoid_: Invite, proposal, request

**Backdrop**:
An optional image the Inviter adds to an Invitation as its background. It belongs to the Invitation, not to the Trip's Photos.
_Avoid_: Background photo, cover

**Inviter**:
The Member who created an Invitation.

**Invitee**:
The Member an Invitation is for: always the other Member.
_Avoid_: Recipient, guest

**Pending**:
An Invitation the Invitee has not yet accepted. While it is pending, its Trip is hidden from the Invitee.

**Accepted**:
An Invitation the Invitee has said "Sí" to. It is frozen from then on and can only be replayed.
_Avoid_: Answered, used, completed
